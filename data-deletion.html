Noodle Uploads Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Noodle Uploads is a private tool used only by its owner, for the owner's own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts. It collects no data about anyone else. The only data it keeps is the owner's own sign-in tokens and a list of the owner's own scheduled posts, stored on the owner's computer.

How to remove access and delete data

  1. Instagram / Facebook (Meta): go to Facebook Settings, then Business Integrations, at facebook.com/settings?tab=business_tools, and remove Noodle Uploads. This revokes its access immediately.
  2. YouTube (Google): remove Noodle Uploads at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  3. TikTok: in the TikTok app, go to Settings and privacy, Security, Manage app permissions, and remove Noodle Uploads.

To have any stored tokens and post records deleted from the owner's computer, email jameshakuy@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion". They will be deleted within 30 days and the deletion confirmed by reply.
