Noodle Uploads Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Noodle Uploads is a private tool used only by its owner. It uploads the owner's own videos and sets their captions, titles, descriptions and publishing schedules on the owner's own accounts on YouTube, Instagram (through Meta) and TikTok. It does nothing else.

What data it uses

It uses only the owner's own account access on those three platforms, to upload the owner's videos and manage their details. It collects no data about anyone else.

Storage

Sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer. They are not sent to or kept on any other server.

Sharing

Data is never sold or shared with anyone.

Google API Services

Noodle Uploads' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

  • YouTube (Google): in your Google Account settings at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Instagram (Meta): in Facebook Settings, under Business Integrations, at facebook.com/settings?tab=business_tools.
  • TikTok: in the TikTok app, under Settings and privacy, Security, Manage app permissions.
