Noodle Uploads Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Noodle Uploads is a private tool used only by its owner. It uploads videos and sets their titles, descriptions and publishing schedules on the owner's own YouTube channels. It does nothing else.

What data it uses

It uses only the owner's own YouTube account access, to upload the owner's videos and manage their details. It collects no data about anyone else.

Storage

Sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer. They are not sent to or kept on any server.

Sharing

Data is never sold or shared with anyone.

Google API Services

Noodle Uploads' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time in your Google Account settings at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
